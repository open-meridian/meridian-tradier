# Tradier's terms

A summary, not legal advice. Read Tradier's own terms before relying on it.

- **Personal use only:** unless you are a Tradier Partner, Tradier's APIs are
  entitled for personal use only.
- **Account holders only:** market data goes only to active Tradier
  Brokerage account holders, and is never redistributed to anyone without
  one.
- **What a personal deployment may keep:** its own copy, for the account
  holder's personal use.

So the plugin declares each dataset's terms as one person's. Your
deployment's Data sources page records the licence you enter, and warns
when more than one person can read a plugin entitled to a one-person
dataset. Open Meridian does not decide whether your use meets Tradier's
terms; the licence records what you entered.

An agent connected to your deployment may pass what it reads to its model's
host; whether that is allowed is for Tradier's terms to say.
